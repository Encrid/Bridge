## Why this exists, said plainly

Bridge is under the GNU Affero General Public License, version 3 only. What is built with it
is under that licence too, and is open.

**Bridge is also offered, by its holder, under other terms that are paid for.** Somebody who
wants to keep closed what they build with Bridge buys those terms. Under them, a product that
contains Bridge, and so contains your contribution, may be passed on without its source.

Only the holder of the rights to all of Bridge can offer it so. This agreement is what gives
the holder that right over your contribution. Without it, a single contributed line would be
a part of Bridge that could be offered under the open licence and under nothing else.

Bridge itself stays under the open licence. Your contribution stays public in it, with its
source. What this agreement adds is that the holder may also license it otherwise, and be paid
for that. You are not paid for it. If that is not acceptable to you, do not sign.

## How to sign

Open a pull request. The first time, a comment on it asks you to answer with this sentence,
and nothing else, as a comment of your own:

    I have read cla.md and I sign it.

Your account's name, the date and the pull request are then recorded on a branch of this
repository, `cla-signatures`. You sign once, and every later contribution you make is covered.
A pull request from somebody who has not signed is marked as failing, and is not merged.
