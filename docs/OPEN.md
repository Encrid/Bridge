# Open items

The register for phase 0. Drafting adds to this and never resolves from it; one closing pass empties
it and promotes the documents out of draft.

Open items only. A decision goes straight into the document it belongs to - never here, or this
becomes a second place to read the design and a second place for it to be wrong. A closed item is
removed, and numbers are never reused, so a gap means something closed.

**Cross-cutting** items must be closed before any document is promoted - more than one document
depends on the answer. **Local** items touch one document and can be closed in the same pass.
