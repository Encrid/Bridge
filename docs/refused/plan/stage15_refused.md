# What was refused: Stage 15

What was weighed in `stage15.md` and refused, each with the condition that would bring it back.

## Outside the tree

Of `stage15.md`, *Outside the tree*.

Refused, after it had been taken: the original's repository renamed to Bridge-Legacy, and this
one given the name it left. One word for many iterations, and a submodule of another project
left pointing at a repository that has not its commit. No condition brings it back.

Refused: this history as a branch of that repository, with `main` left as it is. Nothing is
forced and all of it can be undone, and the library that is Bridge would be a side branch of
the one it replaces. The condition that brings it back is the Rust line being taken up again as
what Bridge is.

Refused: one commit with both histories as its parents and this tree as its content, so that
one log shows all of it. The graph would say this descends from the other, and `readme.md` says
it is its own design and not a rewrite. No condition brings it back.

Refused: the numbers kept, 0.35 to 0.38, as tags. They read as versions of one thing, and the
package says another version. No condition brings it back.

Refused: Enkrid's count kept ahead of the name, `35-cpp-plugins`. It was recommended, and he
ruled against it: the count was not kept after 38, and is not sure before. The condition that
brings it back is the count being written down somewhere it can be checked.

Refused: `old/` for `legacy/`. Either was offered. Legacy is the word for what is kept and no
longer worked on, and old says only when. No condition brings it back.

## What the design before this one is called

Of `stage15.md`, *What the design before this one is called*.

Refused: "the original Bridge", as it was. It is the fourth design and not the first, and an
adjective away from this library's name. No condition brings it back.

Refused, though it was recommended: "the Rust Bridge", said once in `readme.md` beside its
branch. A name in prose and a name in the repository for one thing, held together by one
sentence. Enkrid ruled for the branch's name alone. No condition brings it back.

Refused: "the previous Bridge". True until another follows, and it says nothing of what the
design was. No condition brings it back.
