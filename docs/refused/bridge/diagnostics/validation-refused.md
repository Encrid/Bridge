## The row test

Of `validation.md`, *The row test*.

Refused: the test always, which is what this was, "at that price". The price is a sixth to a third
of the loop this design exists to keep fast, paid by every wasm module, to catch a mistake that only
its author can make and only its author can weigh. The condition that brings it back is the test
costing nothing, as it would if a compiler hoisted it.

Refused: a second family of accessors that tests once a span, beside the first: two to keep equal,
alike to read and different in what they catch. The condition that brings it back is one module that
needs both in one build.
